# 3TABZ: Growth and Market Research

*Prepared 2026-10-22. Every factual claim links to a source. Company-reported numbers say "company claim". Third-party estimates name their source. "Not found" means I searched and found nothing citable.*

> **Method note:** Reddit blocked both direct fetching and domain-restricted search from this environment (SITE_BLOCKED / proxy 400). Pain-point evidence therefore comes from App Store and Trustpilot reviews, Apple Support Community threads with "Me too" counts, a peer-reviewed content analysis of 1,323 medication-app reviews, patient forums (Mayo Clinic Connect) and press. Mention frequencies are rough ordinal estimates drawn from those sources, not Reddit counts. Before launch, run a manual Reddit pass to validate the ranking.

## 1. Executive summary

1. **The incumbent is pulling back outside the US.** Since **1 Jan 2026**, Medisafe has been a *fully paid app outside the US* at $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr ([Medisafe help center](https://help-center.medisafe.com/en/articles/13052285-medisafe-is-now-a-fully-paid-app-for-users-outside-the-u-s)). Its UK Trustpilot reviews are dominated by complaints that previously free features were paywalled (TrustScore 2.0) ([Trustpilot UK](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/medisafe.com)). This leaves a large group of displaced UK, EU, Canadian, Australian and Nigerian users, and it is 3TABZ's best near-term acquisition opening.
2. **Free reminders are table stakes.** Apple Health Medications ships with iOS 16+ and adds follow-up reminders and Critical Alerts in iOS 17 ([iPhone Life](https://iphonelife.com/content/your-medication-reminder-app-iphone-can-now-check-interactions), [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/inside/ios-17/tips/how-to-set-up-follow-up-medication-reminders-in-ios-17)). Samsung Health added meds with Elsevier-sourced interaction checks in Dec 2023 ([Samsung](https://news.samsung.com/us/new-medications-tracking-feature-samsung-health)). MyTherapy is free (company claim: 12M+ users) ([smartpatient](https://smartpatient.eu/blog/10-years-refinement-mytherapy-the-platform-for-pharma-digital-services)). **Never paywall core reminders.**
3. **People pay for what the free apps lack:** caregiver coordination, chronic-condition depth (flares, sickle-cell crises, symptoms linked to doses), doctor-ready output, and reliability. Bearable charges for correlation reports and history beyond 30 days (company claim: 900k+ members) ([Bearable](https://bearable.app/)). Guava charges $8/mo or $78/yr for AI visit prep and family managers ([Guava](https://guavahealth.com/plans)).
4. **Caregiver loops are the best-evidenced growth and retention lever.** In Medisafe's own study, users who added a "Medfriend" were **59% more likely to still be active at 90 days**, and 71% of non-adherent users improved their adherence (company study, 2018) ([Medisafe](https://medisafe.com/education-resources/the-impact-of-medisafes-medfriend-caregiver-feature-on-adherence)). The US has **63M family caregivers**, and over half handle tasks such as medication management ([AARP 2025](https://www.aarp.org/press/releases/2025-07-24-new-report-reveals-crisis-point-for-americas-63-million-family-caregivers.html)).
5. **SEO is how GoodRx scaled.** Semrush estimates goodrx.com at about **28.5M organic visits** in Sep 2026, ranking #1 for "glp-1" and #3 for "pharmacy near me" ([Semrush](https://cw.semrush.com/website/goodrx.com/overview)). Programmatic drug, condition and interaction pages are 3TABZ's main web channel.
6. **Benchmarks for Health & Fitness apps (RevenueCat SOSA 2026):** median D35 download-to-paid is **2.9%** (top quartile 6.2%), trial-to-paid **37.7%**, and yearly price **$39.94** ([RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)). D30 retention for H&F apps is **3–8%** (AppsFlyer and Adjust via [Business of Apps](https://businessofapps.com/data/health-fitness-app-benchmarks)).
7. **950k paying subscribers is not realistic. Plan for 1M users and 40–80k payers.** GoodRx has ~5M monthly active consumers and **674k subscription plans** (Q4 2025) ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2026/goodrx-targets-digital-subscription-growth-amid-14-dip-in-active-consumers/)). Reaching 1M *installs* at median conversion yields about **29k payers**. 950k payers would take roughly 15–33M cumulative installs.
8. **Web billing is legal to promote in the US, nearly everywhere else in Europe, and only quietly elsewhere.** On the Apple US storefront, buttons and links to web checkout are allowed and Apple currently collects no commission. The Supreme Court is reviewing that ([TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/supreme-court-apple-epic-contempt-app-store-commission)). In other regions, guideline 3.1.3(b) lets the app *unlock* a web purchase without mentioning it, as long as IAP is also offered ([Apple guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)).
9. **The Nigeria case is real but must be priced in naira and built Android-first.** Android has **84%** of Nigeria's mobile OS share ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/nigeria)) and smartphone penetration is **75%** ([KPMG via Arise](https://www.arise.tv/kpmg-nigerias-smartphone-penetration-climbs-to-75-as-economy-becomes-mobile-first/)). Out-of-pocket spending is **58.3%** of health expenditure ([NHA via Peoples Gazette](https://gazettengr.com/out-of-pocket-health-spending-in-nigeria-fell-to-58-3-in-2024-report/)). About **150k** babies are born with sickle cell disease each year ([Africa Check](https://www.africacheck.org/fact-checks/reports/weighing-nigerias-sickle-cell-burden-it-worlds-highest)). For a price anchor, Spotify Premium costs about **₦1,600** ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/05/25/spotify-on-nigerias-streaming-economy/)).
10. **Trust is a moat, and privacy failures are fatal in this category.** The FTC fined GoodRx $1.5M for sharing Rx data with Facebook and Google ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2023/02/01/goodrx-ftc-penalty-sharing-health-data-facebook-google)). The no-diagnosis stance keeps 3TABZ under FDA enforcement discretion ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/medicaldevices/digitalhealth/mobilemedicalapplications/ucm368744.htm)) and out of EU MDR Rule 11 class IIa ([OpenRegulatory](https://openregulatory.com/mdcg/mdcg-2019-11)). Red-flag escalation must stay generic ("call emergency services") rather than condition-specific.

**The arithmetic on 950k subscribers:** at a 2.9% to 6.2% conversion rate, 950k payers needs about **15M to 33M installs**, before churn. Comparables:
- GoodRx, with 28M organic visits a month, has 674k subscription plans.
- Bearable claims 900k *members*, not payers.
- Visible claims 200k users.

A credible stretch target for months 24–36 is **1M registered users, 300k MAU, and 40–80k paying subscribers** (about $1.5–3M ARR at about $38 blended). That is a strong outcome. Reaching 950k "actives" is plausible only if B2B2C seats (pharma, employers, payers) count as actives, which is how MyTherapy and Ada got to 12M+ and 50M.

(3) Employer and caregiver benefits, plus payer and pharma sponsored seats (MyTherapy and Ada model) without ads or data sales. (4) Localisation into FR, ES, DE, PT, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo labels | Paying subscribers 40–80k. B2B seats as upside. Net revenue retention ≥ 90% on annual plans |

**Things to avoid:** ad-supported free tiers (privacy risk and conflicts with the no-data-sale brand), points-for-gift-cards gamification (the Mango Health lesson), and dose calculators or condition-specific triage (medical-device risk).

**Answer to "can a web purchase unlock in-app without mentioning the web?"** Yes, on both stores worldwide, as long as the app sells the **same subscription via IAP / Play Billing**.
- Apple 3.1.3(b) requires IAP parity. A forum case shows rejection when an app had no IAP and a web-paid account ([Apple forum](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/828238)).
- Use RevenueCat Web Billing or Stripe with redemption links ([RevenueCat docs](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/web/overview)) and sign-in to restore.
- In US and EU builds, show a "Save with web checkout" button. Gate it by storefront (`Storefront.current` / Play billing-program APIs).
- Health and wellness is not a Reader category, so the 3.1.3(a) link entitlement does not apply.
