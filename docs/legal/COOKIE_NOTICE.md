# 3TABZ Website Cookie Notice

Version 2026-10-16. Applies to 3tabz.com (the public website) and dashboard.3tabz.com (staff only).

**3tabz.com** uses **no cookies and no tracking or analytics scripts** in the pages provided in this release. The password-reset
page sends your new password directly to the 3TABZ API over HTTPS and stores nothing in your browser.
If analytics are added later, they must be off until you accept them, with an equally easy "Decline" button on the homepage
(NDPC GAID 2025), and this notice must be updated first.

**dashboard.3tabz.com** (staff only) uses strictly necessary cookies:

| Cookie | Purpose | Lifetime | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| `__Host-3tabz_admin` | signed-in staff session (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict) | up to 8 hours | strictly necessary |
| `__Host-3tabz_pending` | two-factor sign-in step | 10 minutes | strictly necessary |
| `__Host-3tabz_csrf` | protects against cross-site request forgery | 8 hours | strictly necessary |

Strictly necessary cookies don't need consent. Contact: privacy@3tabz.com
