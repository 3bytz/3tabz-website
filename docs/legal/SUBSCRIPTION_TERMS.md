# 3TABZ Pro — Subscription Terms

**Draft for legal review — version 2026-10-16.**

- **What you get:** full adherence history and trends (beyond 7 days), every pattern from your check-ins, personal daily tips and routine analysis (requires AI consent), appointment summaries from your symptom journal, meal-planner cost estimates and unlimited re-plans, caregiver mode. Reminders, dose logging, safety checks, emergency numbers, interaction checker, symptom journal, doctor reports/links, care partners, data export and deletion are **free**.
- **Plans:** monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscriptions. The price, billing period and any free trial are shown on the paywall **before** you buy, in your local currency as set by Apple or Google.
- **Payment:** charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account at confirmation of purchase.
- **Auto-renewal:** the subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least **24 hours before** the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period.
- **Free trial:** if offered, any unused part of a trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription. You won't be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
- **Cancel / manage:** iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. **Deleting the app or your 3TABZ account does not cancel the subscription.**
- **Refunds:** handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play under their policies. We can't issue store refunds ourselves.
- **Restore:** use "Restore purchases" on the paywall after reinstalling or on a new phone (same Apple ID / Google account).
- **Price changes:** the stores notify you and, where required, ask for your consent before a higher price applies.
- **Family Sharing:** not offered in this version.
- Questions: support@3tabz.com
