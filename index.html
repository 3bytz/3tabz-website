Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Is 3TABZ a medical device? Will it tell me what to take?

No. 3TABZ helps you follow the plan you already have with your clinician. It does not diagnose, prescribe or change doses. If something looks urgent, it points you to emergency services.

Do reminders work without internet?

Yes. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, so they fire on a plane, underground or with no data. Your logs sync when you are back online.

Which countries do you support?

3TABZ works worldwide in English, with local emergency numbers, local currency for meal costs, and region-aware examples. More languages are on the way.

Can my family see my medicines?

Only if you invite them, and only what you allow. You can remove anyone from your care circle in one tap.

How do I cancel Pro?

If you subscribed in the App Store or Google Play, cancel in your store account settings. If you subscribed on this website, open your account page. Your reminders and records stay free either way.

Do you sell or share my data?

No. We do not sell personal data, we do not run ads, and we do not use third-party tracking in the app. Read the privacy policy.
