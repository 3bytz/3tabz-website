01Summary

  • 3TABZ helps you manage medicines and keep a health diary. It does not diagnose, treat or provide telemedicine.
  • We collect the health information you enter so the app can work. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or show ads.
  • AI features are off unless you turn them on. When on, we send only the minimum de-identified details needed.
  • You can export your data or delete your account in the app at any time (Profile → Privacy & Data).

07Your rights (NDPA s.34–38)

You can: access your data and get a copy (Export my data — a one-time download link valid 72 hours); correct it in the app; delete your account (Delete my account) or individual entries; withdraw consent at any time (as easily as you gave it, s.35) — e.g. turn off AI or care-partner sharing; object to processing; not be subject to solely automated decisions with significant effects (3TABZ makes none); and complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng). UK/EU users also have equivalent rights under UK/EU GDPR and may complain to the ICO or their local authority. Email dpo@3tabz.com — we reply within [30] days.

08Security

HTTPS for all traffic; passwords hashed with bcrypt; free-text health notes encrypted field-by-field (AES-256-GCM) on top of storage encryption; sign-in tokens stored in your phone's secure keychain; admin access needs two-factor sign-in and every admin action is logged; optional app lock and discreet notifications. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to risk your rights we will notify the NDPC within 72 hours (s.40) and tell you without undue delay where the risk is high.

09Children

3TABZ accounts are for adults (18+). Parents/guardians may record information about their children as dependants; they are responsible for having the authority to do so. We do not knowingly create accounts for children. Contact us if you think a child has an account.

10Notifications on shared phones

Reminders may show medicine names on your lock screen. Turn on Discreet reminders to hide them.
